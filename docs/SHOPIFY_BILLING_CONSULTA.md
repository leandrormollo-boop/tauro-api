**Asunto:** Free-to-install logistics app with freight billed off-platform — Billing API required?

Hello Partner Support,

We are preparing to submit **Tauro Solutions Ar** (public embedded app,
client_id `c8357c0bfb8cdd371729d8bc321a6bd6`) to the Shopify App Store and want
to confirm our billing classification before submission.

- The app is **free to install** and has no software fee, no in-app purchases
  and no plans.
- TAURO is a logistics provider (freight forwarder / courier broker) in
  Argentina. Merchants who use the app contract **physical shipping services**
  (carrier freight, customs handling) with TAURO. Those services are quoted,
  invoiced and paid **outside Shopify**, under the merchant's commercial
  agreement with TAURO, exactly as they would be without the app.
- The app does not quote shipping rates in checkout (no CarrierService) and does
  not take any payment from merchants.

Could you please confirm in writing whether, under the App Store requirements
(section 1.2 — App Pricing / Billing API), this model is acceptable as a
free-to-install app with off-platform freight billing, or whether Shopify
considers the freight an app charge that must go through the Billing API?
